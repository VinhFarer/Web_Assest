Returns and Exchange Policies

TipToe Soles offers FREE RETURNS for purchases over $60 within 28 days of the order date. Customers are encouraged to use registered post when returning items, as TipToe Soles cannot accept responsibility for returned parcels that are lost in transit.

Conditions for Returns

  • Returns must be made within 28 days of the order date.
  • Items must be unworn and in original condition.
  • All original tags must remain attached.
  • Shoes must be returned in their original shoe box.
  • Products showing signs of wear, marks, or damage will not be accepted.
  • Postage costs are non-refundable.

TipToe Soles reserves the right to reject returns that do not meet the above conditions.

Exchanges

Exchanges may be arranged for eligible items returned within the specified return period, provided all return conditions are satisfied.
